I'm a **Ph.D. student** in the [Dept. of Visual Computing and AI at MPI for Informatics](https://www.mpi-inf.mpg.de/departments/visual-computing-and-artificial-intelligence), advised by [**Dr. Rishabh Dabral**](https://rishabhdabral.github.io/) and [**Prof. Christian Theobalt**](https://people.mpi-inf.mpg.de/~theobalt/). My research focuses on **human motion synthesis** and **human-object-scene interaction** at the intersection of Computer Vision, Computer Graphics, and Machine Learning.

Previously, I completed my Master's degree at [POSTECH Computer Graphics Lab](https://cg.postech.ac.kr) under the supervision of [**Prof. Seung-Hwan Baek**](https://www.shbaek.com), working on **multi-dimensional light properties modeling** ([NeSpoF](https://youngchan-k.github.io/NeSpoF), [spDataset](https://eschoi.com/SPDataset/), [hpBRDF](https://yunseong0518.github.io/projects/hpBRDF/)). I received my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics & Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Yonsei University.
